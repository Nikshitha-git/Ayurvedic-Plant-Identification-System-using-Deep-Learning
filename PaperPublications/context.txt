AyurLeaf is an AI-powered Ayurvedic plant identification system that combines Computer Vision,
Deep Learning, Flask, MongoDB, and a responsive web interface to identify medicinal plants from leaf images 
and provide their corresponding Ayurvedic and medicinal information.

The system allows users to capture or upload a leaf image,
analyze it using a trained YOLO-based object detection model, 
and instantly receive the predicted plant name along with its scientific name, 
Ayurvedic properties, medicinal uses, and traditional knowledge.
